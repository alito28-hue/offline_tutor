The Vikings were warriors from the Norse peoples, mainly from Scandinavia. They were famous as great sailors.

They carried out raids and looting in Europe. Their language was Old Norse.

They became known in Europe when they looted the monastery of Lindisfarne, in northern Britain, in the year 793.
