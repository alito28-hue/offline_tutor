The Vikings were Norse peoples, mostly from Scandinavia, famous as great sailors. Their language was Old Norse.

They made raids and plundered across Europe. They became known for raiding the monastery of Lindisfarne, in northern Britain, in the year 793.

They were also traders: they exchanged furs for silver and Arab spices. The Norwegians settled Iceland and Greenland.
