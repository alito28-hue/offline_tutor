A shadow is a dark area that appears when an object blocks the light. It fills the space behind the object.

The shape of the shadow looks like the outline of the object that blocks the light.

If there are several sources of light, there are several shadows, and the parts where they overlap are darker. The shadow of a person standing joins their feet, at the point where they touch the ground.
