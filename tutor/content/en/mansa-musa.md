Mansa Musa was the tenth mansa of the Mali Empire, in West Africa. He lived from about 1280 to 1337.

The word mansa translates as "king of kings" or "emperor".

He was the richest ruler of his time. When his wealth is adjusted for the passing of time, he is considered the richest person of all time.
