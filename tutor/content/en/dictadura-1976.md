Argentina's last dictatorship ruled from the coup of March 24, 1976 until December 10, 1983.

It carried out a plan of state terrorism: there were kidnappings, torture and people who disappeared, and babies were taken from their families. There were secret detention centers. Human rights organizations speak of 30,000 disappeared people.

Every March 24, the National Day of Remembrance for Truth and Justice, the victims are remembered.
