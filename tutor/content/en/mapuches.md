The Mapuche are the largest Indigenous people of Chile and Argentina. In Argentina there were 145,783 people in 2022, and in Chile, 1,623,073 in 2024.

Their language is Mapuzungun, or Mapudungun. The Spanish called them Araucanians.

They have lived on both sides of the Andes since long before the borders between Argentina and Chile existed.
