The Nile is the largest river in Africa. It flows north through ten countries, including Sudan and Egypt, and ends in the Mediterranean Sea.

There it forms a great delta, where the cities of Cairo and Alexandria stand.

It is 6,853 kilometers long. For centuries it was considered the longest river in the world, but some measurements give that place to the Amazon, and specialists still debate it.
