The Nile is the largest river in Africa. It flows north through ten countries, including Sudan and Egypt, and empties into the Mediterranean Sea.

There it forms a large delta, where the cities of Cairo and Alexandria are. It is 6,853 kilometers long.

For centuries it was thought to be the longest river in the world. Measurements from 2008 say the longest may be the Amazon, but not all scientists agree.
