Roman numerals began in ancient Rome and use capital letters as symbols. I is 1, V is 5, X is 10, L is 50, C is 100, D is 500 and M is 1000.

The values are added when they go from larger to smaller. For example, VI is 5 + 1 = 6, and XX is 10 + 10 = 20.

If a smaller letter comes before a larger one, it is subtracted. For example, IV is 5 - 1 = 4, and IX is 10 - 1 = 9.
