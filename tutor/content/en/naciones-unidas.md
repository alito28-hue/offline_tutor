The United Nations, or UN, is a world organization of countries. It was created with the signing of the UN Charter on June 26, 1945.

Its purpose is to keep international peace and security, encourage friendly relations and promote cooperation among countries.

Its headquarters are in New York City. Its main bodies include the General Assembly and the Security Council.
