The First World War, also called the Great War, lasted from 1914 to 1918.

It set two alliances against each other: the Allies and the Central Powers. It was fought mainly in Europe and the Middle East.

It was one of the deadliest conflicts in history: about 10 million soldiers and another 10 million civilians died.
