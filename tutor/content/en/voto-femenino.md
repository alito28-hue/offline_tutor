Argentine women won the vote in 1947, with Law 13.010, also called the Evita law after Eva Perón.

They voted for the first time on November 11, 1951.

Before that, activists from the Socialist Party and the Radical Civic Union were already demanding it, such as Elvira Rawson, Julieta Lanteri and Alicia Moreau.
