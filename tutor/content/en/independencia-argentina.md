Argentine independence was declared on July 9, 1816, at the Congress of Tucuman.

With that decision, the United Provinces of the Rio de la Plata broke their political dependence on the Spanish monarchy.

It was signed at the House of Tucuman, in San Miguel de Tucuman. Ten days later, the Congress also rejected any other foreign rule.
