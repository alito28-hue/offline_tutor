Mammals are a group of vertebrate animals. They have fur or hair and they control their body temperature very precisely. They are the only animals that produce milk for their young.

Almost all mammals give birth to live young; the monotremes are the exception. All mammals take care of their young, sometimes for a long time.

Some mammals live in the sea, such as whales, seals and dolphins. Humans and other primates are mammals too.
