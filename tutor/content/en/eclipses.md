An eclipse happens when one body in space blocks the light coming from another. It happens when the Sun, the Moon and the Earth line up.

In a solar eclipse, the shadow of the Moon crosses the surface of the Earth. It can only happen during some new moons.

In a lunar eclipse, the Moon moves into the shadow of the Earth. It can only happen during some full moons.
