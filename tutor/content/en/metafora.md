A metaphor is a figure of speech: it names one thing by mentioning another that is like it.

It is used to say something in a more beautiful or stronger way. It joins two different things that have something in common.

Example: in Don Quixote, Cervantes describes Dulcinea and calls her eyes suns and her cheeks roses. Her eyes shine like the sun, so he calls them suns.
