The Conquest of the Desert was a military campaign by Argentina between 1878 and 1885. With it, Argentina took control of large areas of the Pampas and Patagonia, which were the ancestral lands of Indigenous peoples such as the Mapuche and the Tehuelche.

Those peoples lost their lands and their identity, and many people were moved by force. These events are still debated.

The name "desert" is misleading: those lands were home to those peoples. The campaign was led by Julio Argentino Roca.
