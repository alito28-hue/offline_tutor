Peru is a country in the west of South America. It borders Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil, Bolivia and Chile, and it has 2,250 kilometers of coast on the Pacific Ocean.

It has very varied landscapes: the Andes, the desert coast in the west and the Amazon in the east.

Several Andean civilizations lived there, starting with Caral-Supe around 3200 BC. The Inca Empire also had its center in this land.
