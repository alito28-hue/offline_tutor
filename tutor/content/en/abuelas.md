The Grandmothers of Plaza de Mayo are an Argentine human rights organization.

They search for the babies and children taken during the last dictatorship, to return them to their families.

By July 2025 they had restored the identity of 140 grandchildren, and the search goes on. Their president is Estela de Carlotto.
