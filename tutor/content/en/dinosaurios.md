Dinosaurs were animals that appeared more than 230 million years ago. Their name means "terrible lizards".

They were the main vertebrates on land for 135 million years.

About 66 million years ago, a mass extinction ended most of them. Not all of them disappeared: birds evolved from one group of dinosaurs.
