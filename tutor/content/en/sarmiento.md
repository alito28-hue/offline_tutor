Domingo Faustino Sarmiento was born in San Juan in 1811 and died in Asuncion in 1888. He was a politician, writer, teacher and journalist, and president of Argentina from 1868 to 1874.

He stood out for promoting public education and teacher training. He wrote the book Facundo.

His ideas about progress saw gauchos and Indigenous peoples as obstacles, something that is strongly criticized today.
