Raúl Alfonsín was born in Chascomus in 1927 and died in Buenos Aires in 2009. He was a lawyer and a leader of the Radical Civic Union.

In 1983 he took office as president, which ended the dictatorship. Many consider him the father of modern democracy in Argentina.

In 1985 the Trial of the Juntas took place, after the Nunca Más ("Never Again") report by CONADEP.
