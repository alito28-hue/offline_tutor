A monarchy is a form of government in which the highest authority is one person, the monarch, who is the head of state. The word means "rule of one".

In an absolute monarchy, the monarch has all the power.

In a constitutional monarchy, a parliament limits the monarch. In a parliamentary monarchy, the monarch often has only a ceremonial role.
