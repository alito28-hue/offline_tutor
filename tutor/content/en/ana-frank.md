Anne Frank was a young Jewish girl born in Germany in 1929, and a victim of the Holocaust. She died in 1945 in Bergen-Belsen.

Her family moved to Amsterdam when Hitler came to power, and later hid in a secret annex.

She wrote a diary about that life, from 1942 until she was arrested in 1944.
