The Renaissance was a broad cultural movement in Western Europe in the 15th and 16th centuries, between the Middle Ages and the modern age.

It began in the city of Florence, in Italy, and then spread throughout Europe. It stood out in the arts and also renewed the sciences.

Its name comes from reviving elements of classical Greek and Roman culture.
