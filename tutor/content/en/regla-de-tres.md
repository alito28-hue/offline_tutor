The rule of three is used to find an unknown value when three other proportional values are known.

Example: if 2 kilos cost 100 pesos, how much do 5 kilos cost? First step: multiply 5 × 100 = 500. Second step: divide by 2. The result is 250 pesos.

The best known is the simple direct rule of three.
