India is a country in South Asia. It has been the most populous country in the world since 2023: in 2026 it is estimated to have more than 1,476 million people.

It is the seventh largest country on the planet.

It borders the Indian Ocean to the south, the Arabian Sea to the west and the Bay of Bengal to the east. Its neighboring countries are Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Myanmar.
