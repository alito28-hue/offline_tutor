India is a country in South Asia. With more than 1,476 million people, it has been the most populated country in the world since 2023.

It is the seventh largest country on the planet.

It meets the Indian Ocean to the south, the Arabian Sea to the west and the Bay of Bengal to the east. Its neighboring countries are Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh and Myanmar.
