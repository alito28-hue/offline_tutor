The Second World War was a global conflict between 1939 and 1945. It set two alliances against each other, the Allies and the Axis, with most countries taking part.

The Axis was made up of Nazi Germany, Fascist Italy and Imperial Japan.

It was the largest war in history, and a huge number of civilians died. The Holocaust was a Nazi plan of extermination, not a side effect of the war.
