Paraguay is a country in the center of South America. Its capital is Asuncion, one of the oldest cities in the Americas.

It has large forests, waterfalls and wetlands. It is organized into a capital and 17 departments.

It has just over 400,000 square kilometers and almost 6,500,000 people. It was one of the founders of Mercosur.
