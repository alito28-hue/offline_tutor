The National Constitution is the one that governs the Argentine Republic. It was approved in 1853 in the city of Santa Fe by an assembly of representatives of thirteen provinces.

Its purpose was to end the civil wars and achieve national unity with a republican and federal system.

It has been amended several times, and the text from the 1994 reform is in force today. It has a preamble and two parts.
