A sentence is a group of words that expresses a complete idea and makes sense on its own. For example: "That teacher explains grammar well".

A sentence has a subject and a predicate. The subject is who or what the sentence is about. The predicate is what is said about the subject.

To find the subject, look for the verb and ask who does the action. In "The dogs run", who runs? The dogs: that is the subject, and run is the predicate.
