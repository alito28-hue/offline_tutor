When two straight lines come together, they make an angle. The two lines are called the sides of the angle and they meet at a point. An angle shows the space between its sides.

To measure the size of an angle, we use units called degrees, with the symbol °. An acute angle is less than 90°. A right angle is exactly 90°. An obtuse angle is more than 90° but less than 180°.

For example, an angle of 45° is an acute angle, because 45 is less than 90. An angle of 120° is an obtuse angle, because 120 is more than 90 and less than 180.
