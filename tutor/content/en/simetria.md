Symmetry is a feature of shapes that stay the same after certain movements.

Some types are rotational symmetry, in which a figure turns until it looks as it did, and folding symmetry, which forms two equal parts when one turns 180° with respect to the other.

There is also translational symmetry, in which a pattern repeats.
