A computer is a programmable machine that follows instructions to process data and give out information.

It has two essential parts. Hardware is the physical part, such as circuits, cables and the keyboard. Software is what you cannot touch, such as programs and data.

It has at least one central processing unit, or CPU, a memory and input and output devices. The keyboard, for example, lets you enter data.
