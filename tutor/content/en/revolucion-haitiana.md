The Haitian Revolution was a movement in the French colonies between 1791 and 1803. It achieved the abolition of slavery in Saint-Domingue in 1793.

It began in 1791 with great uprisings of enslaved people in the north of the colony.

Toussaint Louverture was one of its main leaders. In 1804 the First Empire of Haiti was proclaimed.
