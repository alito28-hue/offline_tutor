Raising to a power is an operation with two terms: the base and the exponent. The exponent shows how many times the base is multiplied by itself.

Example: 2 to the power of 3 is 2 × 2 × 2 = 8.

The exponent 2 is read "squared" and 3 is read "cubed". For example, 5 squared is 5 × 5 = 25.
