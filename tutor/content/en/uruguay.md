Uruguay is a country in South America, in the east of the Southern Cone. Its capital and most populated city is Montevideo.

It borders Brazil to the northeast and Argentina to the west and southwest, separated by the Rio de la Plata. To the south is the Atlantic Ocean.

It is the second smallest country in South America, after Suriname. It had 3,499,451 people in the 2023 census.
