Indonesia is a country in Southeast Asia, between the Indian and Pacific oceans. It has more than 17,000 islands and it is the largest island state in the world.

With more than 280 million people, it is the fourth most populated country in the world.

Java is the most populated island in the world, and more than half of the country's people live there.
