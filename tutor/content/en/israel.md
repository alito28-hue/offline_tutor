Israel is a country in Western Asia, in the Middle East, with a coast on the Mediterranean Sea.

It borders Lebanon, Syria, Jordan and Egypt, as well as the West Bank and the Gaza Strip. It has almost 10 million people, and most are Jewish.

Its seat of government is in Jerusalem, a city whose status is under international debate.
