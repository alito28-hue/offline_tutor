Palestine is a country in Asia, in the Middle East. It is made up of the regions of the West Bank and the Gaza Strip, and it has 6,520 square kilometers.

The West Bank borders Israel and Jordan. Gaza borders Egypt, Israel and the Mediterranean Sea.

Not all countries recognize it as a state.
