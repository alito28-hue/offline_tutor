Mohandas Gandhi was born in India in 1869. He was the most important leader of India's independence movement against British rule.

He was a lawyer, politician and pacifist. Rabindranath Tagore gave him the name Mahatma, which means "great soul".

He rejected armed struggle. He taught nonviolence and civil disobedience, which means peacefully refusing to obey unfair laws.

He defended Muslims during the religious conflicts that followed independence. He was murdered by a fanatic on January 30, 1948, in New Delhi.
