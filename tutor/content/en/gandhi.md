Mohandas Gandhi was born in India in 1869 and died in 1948. He was the most prominent leader of the movement for India's independence from British rule.

He was a lawyer, politician and pacifist. Rabindranath Tagore gave him the name Mahatma, which means "great soul".

He rejected armed struggle. He taught non-violence and civil disobedience, which means peacefully refusing to obey unfair laws.
