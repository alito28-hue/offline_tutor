Perimeter is the distance around a flat shape. For a polygon, it is the sum of the lengths of all its sides.

How to find the perimeter of a rectangle: add its four sides, or use P = 2 x length + 2 x width. Example: a rectangle 5 cm long and 3 cm wide has a perimeter of 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16 cm.
