Tango is a musical genre and a dance from the Rio de la Plata region, mainly the cities of Buenos Aires and Montevideo.

It has many different roots: African, gaucho, Spanish and Italian, along with the contribution of the great wave of immigrants from Europe.

Today it is one of the best-known musical genres around the world.
