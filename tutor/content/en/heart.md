The heart is a fist-sized muscle that pumps blood through tubes called blood vessels. The heart and the vessels make up the circulatory system. Blood brings oxygen and food to the body and takes carbon dioxide to the lungs.

The human heart has four chambers: two atria at the top and two ventricles at the bottom. Blood goes through the heart in one direction. Special flaps called valves stop the blood from going backward.

A heart beat is when the heart muscle contracts. Step 1: the heart squeezes and pushes blood out. Step 2: the muscle relaxes and the heart fills with blood again.
