Juan Domingo Perón was born in Lobos in 1895 and died in Olivos in 1974. He was a soldier and politician, and three times president of Argentina. He founded Peronism, or Justicialism.

He was the first president elected by the votes of both men and women. His governments expanded workers' rights and promoted industry.

He was overthrown by a coup in 1955, his party was banned for years, and he became president again in 1973.
