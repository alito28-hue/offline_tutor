The pyramids of Egypt were built as royal tombs for the pharaohs, starting around 2630 BC.

They were made with stone blocks covered in limestone, so they were originally white.

The best known are those of Khufu, Khafre and Menkaure, on the Giza plain, on the outskirts of Cairo. Khufu's is the tallest.
