Hipólito Yrigoyen was twice president of Argentina, from 1916 to 1922 and from 1928 to 1930, and an important figure of the Radical Civic Union.

He was the first president elected under the Sáenz Peña Law of 1912, which made voting secret and compulsory for men. Women could not vote yet.

He was overthrown by a coup in 1930.
