The conquest of the Americas was the process by which European states took over the continent, with military conflicts and policies to bring those lands and the Indigenous peoples under their rule.

It began with Christopher Columbus in 1492.

For Indigenous peoples it meant a huge fall in their population, because of wars and diseases brought from Europe.
