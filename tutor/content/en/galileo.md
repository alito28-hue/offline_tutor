Galileo Galilei was an Italian astronomer, engineer, mathematician and physicist. He was born in Pisa in 1564 and died in 1642.

He improved the telescope, made many astronomical observations, stated the first law of motion and supported the ideas of Copernicus.

He is considered the father of modern astronomy, modern physics and modern science.
