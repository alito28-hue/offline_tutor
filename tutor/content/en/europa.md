Europe is a continent in the Northern Hemisphere. It goes from the Arctic Ocean in the north to the Mediterranean Sea in the south, and to the Atlantic Ocean in the west.

To the east, the Ural Mountains, the Ural River, the Caspian Sea, the Caucasus and the Black Sea separate it from Asia.

It is the second smallest continent, with about 10.5 million square kilometers.
