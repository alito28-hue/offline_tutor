A prime number is a natural number greater than 1 that can only be divided exactly by 1 and by itself. Examples: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11 and 13.

Numbers that have other divisors are called composite. For example, 6 is composite because it can be divided by 2 and by 3.

The number 1 is neither prime nor composite. The number 2 is prime and it is the only even prime.
