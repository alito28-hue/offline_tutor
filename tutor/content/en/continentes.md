A continent is a large area of land, separated from others by oceans or by geographic, cultural and historical differences.

The word comes from Latin and means "to hold together", because of the continuous land.

How many continents there are depends on the model used. Some separate Europe and Asia, and others count them as one continent, Eurasia. The idea of a continent is a convention created by people.
