Marie Curie was born in Warsaw in 1867 and died in 1934. She was a Polish, later French, physicist and chemist.

She was a pioneer in the study of radioactivity.

She is the first and only person to receive two Nobel Prizes in different sciences: Physics and Chemistry. She was also the first woman professor at the University of Paris.
