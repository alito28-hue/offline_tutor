France is a country in Western Europe, and it is one of the 27 states of the European Union. It also has overseas territories in the Americas and in the Atlantic, Pacific and Indian oceans.

Its capital and largest city is Paris. Other important cities are Marseille, Lyon, Toulouse and Bordeaux.

In Europe it goes from the Rhine River to the Atlantic Ocean, and from the Mediterranean Sea and the Pyrenees to the English Channel and the North Sea.
