Colonialism is the system by which a foreign state rules and exploits another land. Those lands are called colonies.

Usually the ruling power wants to make use of their natural resources and their people.

Examples: Spanish and Portuguese rule in the Americas, between the 16th and 19th centuries, and British and French rule in Africa and Asia, in the 19th and 20th centuries.
