Percent means out of one hundred. It is shown with the symbol %. A percentage is one way of writing a ratio; you can also write it as a fraction or a decimal.

To find a percentage, step 1: write the part out of the whole as a fraction. Step 2: change it to a fraction out of 100. For example, a bowl of fruit with three apples and one orange has 3 apples out of 4 pieces of fruit, and 3/4 = 75/100 = 75%.

Percentages are useful because they let you compare things that are not out of the same number. For example, exam marks are often percentages, so you can compare them even if one exam has more questions than the other.
