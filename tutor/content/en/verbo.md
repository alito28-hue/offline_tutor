A verb is a word that shows an action, a process or a state.

Examples: in "Juan climbs the stairs", climbs is an action. In "Ramon has grown", has grown is a process. In "Ramon is tired", is shows a state.

A verb changes form depending on who does the action and when: I sing, they sang. To find it, ask yourself what the subject does or how it is.
