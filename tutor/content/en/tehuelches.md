The Tehuelche, or Aonikenk, are an Indigenous community of Patagonia who live mainly in Argentina. The name Tehuelche groups several communities of Patagonia and the Pampas.

Magellan called the ones he met in 1520 "Patagones".

At the end of the 19th century, the advance of the Argentine state over Patagonia took away much of their land.
