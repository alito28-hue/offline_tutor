Volume is the measure of the space a body takes up, in three dimensions.

In a box it is found by multiplying three lengths: the length, the width and the height.

Example: a box 5 cm long, 2 cm wide and 3 cm high has a volume of 5 × 2 × 3 = 30 cubic centimeters.
