Simón Bolívar was born in Caracas in 1783 and died in Santa Marta in 1830. He was a Venezuelan soldier and politician.

He was a key leader of the independence of what are now Venezuela, Colombia, Ecuador and Panama, and one of the main architects of that of Peru.

That is why he received the title of Liberator.
