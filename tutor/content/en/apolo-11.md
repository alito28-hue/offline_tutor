Apollo 11 was the United States mission that first took humans to the Moon, in July 1969.

Its crew were commander Neil Armstrong, Michael Collins and Edwin "Buzz" Aldrin.

It took off on July 16 from the Kennedy Space Center, in Florida, on a Saturn V rocket.
