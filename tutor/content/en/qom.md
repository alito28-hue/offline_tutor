The Qom, also known as Toba, are a people of the Central Chaco.

Since the 16th century they have lived in northern Argentina, in provinces such as Chaco, Formosa and Salta.

During the 20th century, many families moved to cities such as Resistencia, Rosario and Greater Buenos Aires, looking for the work and land they lacked in their home areas.
