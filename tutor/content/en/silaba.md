A syllable is a group of sounds said in a single beat of your voice.

Most of the time it has a vowel in the center, and it can have consonants before or after it.

Words can be split into syllables. For example, the Spanish word bueno has two syllables: bue and no. To split a word, say it slowly and listen for each beat.
