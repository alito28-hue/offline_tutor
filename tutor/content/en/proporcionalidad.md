Two quantities are directly proportional when, if one is multiplied by a number, the other is multiplied by the same number.

If you divide one quantity by the other, you always get the same result. That number is called the constant of proportionality.

Example: if 1 candy costs 5 pesos, 2 candies cost 10 and 3 candies cost 15. The price divided by the amount is always 5.
