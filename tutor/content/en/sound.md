Sound is caused by waves of pressure called sound waves. All sounds are made by vibrations. For example, when a person hits a drum, the drum vibrates and makes the air move.

Sound travels to us in order. Step 1: an object vibrates. Step 2: the vibrations make air molecules move, and sound waves travel away. Step 3: the moving air reaches our ears and the eardrum vibrates too, so we hear the sound.

Sound can travel through solids, liquids and gases. Sound travels fastest through solids, because the particles are closer together. Sound cannot go through a vacuum, such as outer space, because there is nothing there to vibrate.
