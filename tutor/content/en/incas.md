The Inca Empire was the largest empire in the Americas before the Europeans arrived. It was also called Tahuantinsuyo, which in Quechua means "land of the four parts".

It arose in the Andes of Peru, from the city of Cuzco. At its largest it covered about 2 million square kilometers, from today's Colombia to Chile.

Its end began with a civil war between Huascar and Atahualpa, and with diseases such as smallpox, brought by the Europeans. In 1532, Francisco Pizarro's Spanish conquerors captured and executed Atahualpa. Inca resistance went on until 1572.
