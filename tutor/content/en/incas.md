The Inca Empire was the largest empire in the Americas before the arrival of the Europeans. It was also called Tawantinsuyu, which in Quechua means "region of the four parts".

It arose in the Andes of Peru, between the 15th and 16th centuries, starting from the city of Cusco.

At its largest it covered about 2 million square kilometers, from what is now Colombia to Chile.
