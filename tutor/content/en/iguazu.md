Iguazu Falls are on the Iguazu River, between the province of Misiones in Argentina and the state of Parana in Brazil.

They are made up of 275 waterfalls. Of these, 80% are on the Argentine side.

The highest waterfall with the most water is the Devil's Throat, at 82 meters. The border between the two countries passes through it.
