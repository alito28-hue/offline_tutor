The Middle Ages are the period of Western civilization between the 5th and 15th centuries. They come after antiquity and before the modern age.

By convention they begin in 476, with the fall of the Western Roman Empire.

Their end is placed in 1492, with Columbus reaching the Americas, or in 1453, with the fall of Constantinople.
