Ancient Egypt was a civilization of antiquity that began along the Nile River. Its history covers more than 3,000 years.

It had two parts: Upper Egypt in the south and Lower Egypt in the north, which were separate lands before they joined.

At its greatest expansion it controlled land in what are now Syria, Palestine and Sudan, and it had great cultural influence on neighboring peoples.
