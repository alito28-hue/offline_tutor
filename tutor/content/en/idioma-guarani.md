Guarani is a language of the Tupi-Guarani family. In its own language it is called avañe'ẽ.

About 9 million people in the Southern Cone speak it, including 6.5 million in Paraguay.

It is one of the two official languages of Paraguay, along with Spanish, under the 1992 Constitution.
