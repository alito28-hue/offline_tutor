The Mongol Empire was the largest empire of connected lands in history. It was founded by Genghis Khan in 1206.

The warrior Temujin united all the Mongol tribes and was proclaimed Great Khan with the name Genghis Khan.

It reached about 23 million square kilometers and more than 100 million people, and it included regions such as China, Persia and Russia.
