Germany is a country in central Europe. It is the European Union state with the most people. Its capital is Berlin.

It is a parliamentary and federal republic, made up of 16 federal states.

It borders Denmark, Poland, the Czech Republic, Austria, Switzerland, France, Luxembourg, Belgium and the Netherlands. To the north are the North Sea and the Baltic Sea.
