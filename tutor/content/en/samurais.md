The samurai were men of the warrior aristocracy of old Japan. Their origin dates from around the 10th century.

They grew stronger at the end of the 12th century, when a military government was created under the shogun. The emperor became almost a spectator.

Their peak was the Sengoku period, a time of constant struggles between clans.
