The Berlin Wall was a wall that formed part of the border between the two Germanys, from August 13, 1961 to November 9, 1989.

It separated West Berlin, linked to the Federal Republic of Germany, from East Berlin, capital of the German Democratic Republic.

It is the best-known symbol of the Cold War and of the division of Germany.
