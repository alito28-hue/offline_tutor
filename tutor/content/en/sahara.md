The Sahara is the largest hot desert in the world. It covers more than 9,400,000 square kilometers.

It fills most of northern Africa, a third of the continent. It goes from the Red Sea to the Atlantic Ocean.

Its name comes from an Arabic word that means desert. Some of its sand dunes reach 193 meters high.
