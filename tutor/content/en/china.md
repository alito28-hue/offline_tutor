China is a country in East Asia with its capital in Beijing. It is the second most populated country in the world: it was first until 2023, when India passed it.

It is divided into 22 provinces, 5 autonomous regions, 4 municipalities and 2 special administrative regions, Hong Kong and Macau.

It has one of the largest economies in the world and it is governed by the Communist Party of China.
