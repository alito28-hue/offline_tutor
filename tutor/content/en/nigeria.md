Nigeria is a country in West Africa. It borders Niger, Chad, Cameroon and Benin, and its coast is on the Gulf of Guinea, in the Atlantic Ocean.

Its capital is Abuja. It is a federation of 36 states and one federal territory.

It has about 243 million people and 250 ethnic groups. The largest are the Hausa and Fulani, the Yoruba and the Igbo.
