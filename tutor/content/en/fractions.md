A fraction shows parts of a whole. The numerator, on top, tells how many parts you take. The denominator, on the bottom, tells how many equal parts the whole is divided into.

How to add fractions with the same denominator: add the numerators and keep the same denominator. Example: 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7.

How to add fractions with different denominators: first find equivalent fractions that share the same denominator, then add the numerators. Example: 1/2 + 1/4. Since 1/2 equals 2/4, you get 2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4.

After adding fractions, you can sometimes simplify the answer. For example, 2/4 is the same as 1/2.
