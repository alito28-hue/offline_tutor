Nouns are words that name people, animals, things, places and ideas. Examples: table, dog, Argentina, joy.

Most English nouns have a singular and a plural form. For example, dog is singular and dogs is plural.

Common nouns name any member of a group, like city. Proper nouns name one in particular, like Cordoba, and they begin with a capital letter.
