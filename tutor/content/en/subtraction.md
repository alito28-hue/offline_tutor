Subtraction finds the difference between two numbers. It uses the minus sign (−). For example, 7 − 4 = 3 can be read as "seven take away four leaves three".

The numbers in a subtraction have names: minuend − subtrahend = difference. In 7 − 4 = 3, the minuend is 7, the subtrahend is 4 and the difference is 3. If the minuend is smaller than the subtrahend, the difference is a negative number: 17 − 25 = −8.

Subtraction is how a cash register finds your change. Step 1: take the amount you paid. Step 2: subtract the cost. For example, if you pay 10 and the cost is 6, your change is 10 − 6 = 4. The order matters in subtraction: 7 − 4 is not the same as 4 − 7.
