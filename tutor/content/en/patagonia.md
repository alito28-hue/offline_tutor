Patagonia is a region in the far south of the Americas. It covers land in Argentina and in Chile.

It has two areas. To the west is the Andean area, with a wet climate and forests. To the east is the Patagonian plateau, with a desert climate and steppes.

It was earlier home to peoples such as the Tehuelche, the Puelche and, in Tierra del Fuego, the Yaghan and the Selknam.
