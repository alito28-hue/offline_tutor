The cardinal points are four directions: north, south, east and west. They help you find your way on a map or on the ground.

East is roughly the side where the Sun rises each day. West is the side where it sets.

Between them are northeast, southeast, southwest and northwest. Together these directions form the compass rose, used for centuries to navigate.
