Ghana is a country in West Africa. Its capital and largest city is Accra.

It borders Burkina Faso, Togo and Ivory Coast, and to the south it meets the Gulf of Guinea.

Its first kingdoms were Dagbon, in the north, and Bonoman, in the south. Later the Ashanti Empire arose.
