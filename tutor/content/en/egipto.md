Egypt is a country in the far northeast of Africa, with one part in Asia: the Sinai Peninsula.

It borders Sudan, Libya, Israel and the Gaza Strip, and it has coasts on the Mediterranean Sea and the Red Sea.

Most of its surface is the Sahara Desert. The Nile River crosses it from south to north and forms fertile land, where many people live.
