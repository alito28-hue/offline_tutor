Climate is the usual condition of the atmosphere in a place, worked out from data covering many years, 30 or more.

Weather, on the other hand, describes what happens over a short time, like today or this week.

To study climate, people look at temperature, pressure, wind, humidity and rainfall. It depends, among other things, on latitude, altitude and distance from the sea.
