The Falklands War, called the Malvinas War in Argentina, was a conflict between Argentina and the United Kingdom in 1982. It lasted about ten weeks and was fought over sovereignty of the Malvinas or Falkland Islands, South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands. It began on April 2, when Argentine troops landed on the islands.

Argentina was then ruled by a dictatorship. Most Argentine soldiers were young conscripts.

The war ended on June 14, 1982 with Argentina's surrender, and the defeat sped up the end of the dictatorship. Every April 2, Argentina honors its veterans and fallen soldiers.
