A food chain shows the feeding relationship between different living things in a habitat. It shows who eats what, and how energy passes from one living thing to another.

A food chain passes energy along in order. Step 1: the sun gives energy to producers, such as plants, which make their food with photosynthesis. Step 2: consumers, which are animals, eat the producers or other animals. Step 3: decomposers such as fungi get energy from what the consumers leave.

In any ecosystem, many food chains overlap, because several consumers may eat the same plant or animal. When this happens, the food chains form a food web. An example of a food chain is grass, rabbit, fox.
