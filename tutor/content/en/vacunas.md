A vaccine prepares the body to defend itself against a disease.

It contains something like the germ that causes the disease, often weakened or dead. The body recognizes it as a threat, destroys it and remembers it.

If the real germ appears later, the body recognizes it and destroys it more easily. Vaccination is the most effective way to prevent infectious diseases.
