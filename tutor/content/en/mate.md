Mate is a drink made with leaves of yerba mate, a plant native to the upper basins of the Paraguay, Parana and Uruguay rivers.

The leaves are dried, cut and ground. They taste bitter, although mate can also be sweetened.

People in the Americas have drunk it since before the Europeans arrived, such as the peoples of the Tupi-Guarani family. Later the Spanish and Portuguese colonizers adopted it.
