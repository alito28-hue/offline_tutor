A lever is a simple machine: a rigid bar that can turn around a point of support, called the fulcrum.

It is used to increase the force applied to an object or to increase its speed or the distance it travels.

To study a lever, you look at three things: the force applied, the resistance and the point of support. People have used levers since prehistoric times.
