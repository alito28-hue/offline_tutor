The Mali Empire was a state in West Africa that existed between 1235 and 1546. Its center was between the present-day cities of Bamako, in Mali, and Siguiri, in Guinea.

It was founded by Sundiata Keita. It was known for the generosity and wealth of its rulers, especially Mansa Musa.

It had a deep influence on West Africa: its language, laws and customs spread along the Niger River.
