The Russian Revolution is the series of events in 1917 that ended the rule of the tsar.

First, the February Revolution forced the tsar to step down and led to a Provisional Government.

Then, in the October Revolution, the Bolsheviks led by Lenin took power and proclaimed the socialist republic. Later the Soviet Union was created.
