Colombia is a country in the northwest of South America. Its capital is Bogota, and it is organized into 32 departments and a capital district.

It is the only country in South America with coasts on both the Pacific and Atlantic oceans.

It has more than 53 million people. Spanish is the official language, and the Constitution also protects the languages of ethnic groups.
