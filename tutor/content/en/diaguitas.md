Diaguita is the name the Incas and later the Spanish gave to independent peoples who spoke a common language, Kakan.

They live in northwestern Argentina, especially in the Calchaqui Valleys, and in northern Chile.

Their ancestors worked metals and made pottery, and Diaguita communities exist today.
