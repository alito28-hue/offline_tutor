The Argentine civil wars took place between 1814 and 1880 and set two groups against each other: the Federalists and the Unitarians.

The Federalists wanted a federal republic with self-governing provinces. The Unitarians wanted a republic with a strong central government in Buenos Aires.

One of the biggest disagreements was how to share power and the income of the Buenos Aires port customs house among the provinces.
