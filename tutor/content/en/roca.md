Julio Argentino Roca was born in Tucuman in 1843 and died in Buenos Aires in 1914. He was a soldier and politician, and president from 1880 to 1886 and from 1898 to 1904.

He led the Conquest of the Desert, for which he is criticized today. He was the main figure of the so-called Generation of '80, and some authors consider him the founder of modern Argentina.

During his first presidency, Law 1420 on free, secular and universal education was passed.
