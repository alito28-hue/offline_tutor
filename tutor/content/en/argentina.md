Argentina is a country in the far south and southeast of South America.

It is a federal country made up of 23 provinces and the Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, which is the federal capital.

Its government is democratic, federal, representative and republican. The provinces have their own executive, legislative and judicial powers, their own highest law and their own flag.
