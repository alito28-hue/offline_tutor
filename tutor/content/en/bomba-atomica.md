Hiroshima and Nagasaki are two cities in Japan that suffered atomic bomb attacks by the United States, on August 6 and 9, 1945.

Together with the Soviet-Japanese war, those attacks contributed to Japan's surrender and the end of the Second World War.

A huge number of civilians died, and radiation damaged the health of the survivors for years.
