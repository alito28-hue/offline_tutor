Ethiopia is a country in the Horn of Africa, once known as Abyssinia. Its capital and largest city is Addis Ababa.

It has no coast. It is the most populated landlocked country in the world and the second most populated in Africa.

It borders Eritrea, Djibouti, Somalia, Kenya, Sudan and South Sudan.
