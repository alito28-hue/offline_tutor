Juana Azurduy was born in 1780 in La Plata, now Sucre, in present-day Bolivia, and she died there in 1862.

She fought in the wars of independence against the Spanish monarchy.

Together with her husband, Manuel Ascensio Padilla, she led an army. Later she took command of the guerrillas of La Laguna. She is honored in both Argentina and Bolivia.
