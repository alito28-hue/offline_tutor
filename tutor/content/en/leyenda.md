A legend is a story about natural or supernatural events, or a mix of both. It is passed from generation to generation, by word of mouth or in writing.

It takes place in a time and place similar to those of the community that tells it. That makes it seem more believable. It can include miracles or fantastic creatures.

As it passes from person to person, a legend changes. That is why there are often different versions of the same story.
