Synonyms are words that have the same or a similar meaning and are the same kind of word. For example, synonyms of disaster are calamity, ruin and catastrophe.

To check, swap one word for the other in a sentence. If the sentence means the same, they are synonyms.

A word can be a synonym of another only in some sentences, because many words have more than one meaning.
