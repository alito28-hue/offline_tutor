The internet is a set of communication networks connected to each other around the world. It works as a single network thanks to common rules called protocols.

It began in 1969, when computers at three universities in California were connected in a network called ARPANET.

The web is not the same as the internet: it is a service created in 1990 that uses the internet to look at pages. Email and other services also work over the internet.
