Cleopatra VII was the last ruler of the Ptolemaic dynasty of ancient Egypt. She lived between 69 and 30 BC.

She was also a diplomat, naval commander, linguist and writer. Her mother tongue was Greek.

After her death, Egypt became a province of the Roman Empire.
