The United States is a federal republic made up of 50 states and a federal district. Its capital is Washington, D.C., and its most populated city is New York.

Most of the country is in the middle of North America, between the Pacific and Atlantic oceans. It borders Canada to the north and Mexico to the south.

Alaska is in the northwest of the continent, separated from Russia by the Bering Strait. Hawaii is an archipelago in Polynesia.
