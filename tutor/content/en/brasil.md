Brazil is a country in South America that covers the eastern half of the subcontinent. Its capital is Brasilia and its most populated city is Sao Paulo.

It has more than 8,500,000 square kilometers: it is the fifth largest country in the world.

To the east is the Atlantic Ocean. It borders French Guiana, Suriname, Guyana, Venezuela, Colombia, Peru, Bolivia, Paraguay, Argentina and Uruguay.
