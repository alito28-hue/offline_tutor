The Industrial Revolution was a great economic, social and technological change that began in Great Britain in the second half of the 18th century.

It began with the practical use of the steam engine improved by James Watt. Then it spread to much of Western Europe and North America.

The economy went from being based on farming to being based on factories and machines. Many workers left the countryside for the cities, which grew quickly.
