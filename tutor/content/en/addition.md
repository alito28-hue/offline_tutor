Addition combines two or more numbers into a new number called the sum. It uses the plus sign (+). For example, if one group has 3 objects and another group has 4, together they have 3 + 4 = 7 objects.

Addition has useful rules. The order does not matter, so 3 + 4 is the same as 4 + 3. Adding 0 does not change a number. When you add more than two numbers, you can add them in any order. The numbers you add are called addends.

To add 3 + 4 by counting, step 1: start at 3. Step 2: count on 4 more numbers: 4, 5, 6, 7. Step 3: the last number you say, 7, is the sum. Repeated addition of 1 is the same as counting.
