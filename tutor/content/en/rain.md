Rain is water that falls from clouds. It is one kind of precipitation, like hail, sleet and snow. Rain is part of the water cycle.

Why it rains: when the Sun heats the ground, the ground heats the air above it. The warm air rises and cools. When it cools enough, clouds form and rain follows.
