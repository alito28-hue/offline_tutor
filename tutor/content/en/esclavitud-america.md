Slavery existed in the Americas before and after the arrival of the Europeans.

In 1530, Spain officially banned enslaving Indigenous people, although some kept doing it illegally. People from Africa and other places were also enslaved.

Millions of people lost their freedom and were forced to work on plantations and in mines.
