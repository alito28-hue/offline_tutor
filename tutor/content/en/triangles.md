A triangle is a shape with three straight sides and three vertices. It is the polygon with the fewest possible sides. The three angles of a triangle always add up to 180°.

Triangles can be grouped by their sides. An equilateral triangle has three sides of the same length. An isosceles triangle has two sides of the same length. A scalene triangle has three sides of different lengths.

Triangles can also be grouped by their angles. A right triangle has a 90° angle, an obtuse triangle has an angle larger than 90° and an acute triangle has only angles of less than 90°. For example, if two angles are 90° and 60°, the third angle is 180 − 90 − 60 = 30°.
