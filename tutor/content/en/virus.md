A virus is a microscopic infectious agent. It can only multiply inside the cells of living things.

It is made of long molecules of DNA or RNA, surrounded by proteins.

When it infects a cell, it forces the cell to make new viruses. Viruses infect all kinds of organisms, such as animals, plants and fungi.
