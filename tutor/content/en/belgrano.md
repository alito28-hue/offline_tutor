Manuel Belgrano was born in Buenos Aires in 1770 and died there in 1820. He was a lawyer, economist, journalist, politician and soldier.

He defended Buenos Aires during the British invasions of 1806 and 1807, and he promoted the May Revolution.

He was a member of the First Junta in 1810 and fought in the Argentine War of Independence.

He created the Argentine flag in 1812.
