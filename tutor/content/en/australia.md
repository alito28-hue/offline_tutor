Australia is a country made up of mainland Australia, the island of Tasmania and many smaller islands. It is the largest country in Oceania and the sixth largest in the world.

It is the flattest and driest inhabited continent. It has deserts in the center, tropical forests in the northeast and mountain ranges in the southeast.

The ancestors of Aboriginal Australians began arriving from Southeast Asia about 65,000 years ago.
