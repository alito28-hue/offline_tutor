Gravity is a pull between any two objects with mass. It is the force which causes objects to fall to the ground. Things fall because the gravity of the Earth pulls on them.

Gravity changes your weight, but not your mass. Mass is the amount of matter in an object, and it stays the same. Weight changes when the pull of gravity changes. An object on the Moon weighs one sixth of its weight on Earth, because the pull of gravity there is weaker.

Gravity also keeps the Solar System together. Newton described how gravity works with his laws, and Einstein explained it with his theory of general relativity.
