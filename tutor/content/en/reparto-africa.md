The Scramble for Africa was the invasion, division and colonization of most of the continent by European powers, between 1881 and 1914.

In 1870 they controlled 10% of Africa, and by 1914, almost 90%. Only Ethiopia and Liberia remained independent. The Berlin Conference of 1884 set rules for that process.

The powers drew borders without respecting the peoples, languages or history of each place.
