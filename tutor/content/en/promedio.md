The average, also called the mean, sums up a group of values in a single number.

How to work it out: add all the values and divide the result by how many values there are. Example with the grades 6, 8 and 10: 6 + 8 + 10 = 24, and 24 divided by 3 is 8. The average is 8.

A value much larger or smaller than the others can change the average a lot.
