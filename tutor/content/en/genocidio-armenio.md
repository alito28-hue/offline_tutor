The Armenian genocide was the forced deportation and extermination of about 1.5 million Armenians in the Ottoman Empire, between 1915 and 1923.

It was carried out by the Young Turk government, mostly during the First World War.

In Argentina, Law 26.199 remembers its victims every April 24.
