In Spanish, the tilde is the small mark written over a vowel, as in camión. It is also called a written accent.

Every Spanish word has one syllable that sounds stronger, but not every word has the mark. As a rule, words with one syllable do not have it.

Some one-syllable words have the mark so that they can be told apart from others. For example: "él" means he and "el" means the.
