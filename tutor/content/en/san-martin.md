José de San Martín was born in Yapeyu in 1778 and died in France in 1850. He was a soldier and politician.

He is known as the liberator of Argentina and Chile, and he was one of the main leaders of the independence of Peru.

As a child he went to live in Spain, where he studied and made a career in the army. He is one of the most important figures of the wars of independence in the Americas, along with Simón Bolívar.
