The Colosseum is an amphitheater of the Roman Empire, built in the 1st century. It was the largest one built in the Empire.

It was called the Flavian Amphitheater. Since the Middle Ages it has been called the Colosseum because a great statue, the Colossus of Nero, stood beside it.

It was built with blocks of travertine, concrete and wood, among other materials.
