The gaucho is the typical inhabitant of the plains and nearby areas of southern South America.

There are gauchos in Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay, southern Brazil, southern Chile and southern Bolivia, even in mountain areas.

A gaucho is known as a skilled rider and for working with cattle. Gaucho life was tied to eating beef and using leather.
