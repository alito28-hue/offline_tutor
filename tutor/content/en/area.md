Area is the amount of space a flat surface takes up. Some units used to measure area are the square metre, the square foot and the square kilometre. The area of an irregular shape can be estimated with grid paper.

To find the area of a rectangle, multiply the length by the width. Step 1: measure the two sides. Step 2: multiply them. For example, a rectangle 5 cm long and 3 cm wide has an area of 5 × 3 = 15 square centimetres.

To find the area of a triangle, take half of the base times the height. For example, a triangle with a base of 6 cm and a height of 4 cm has an area of 6 × 4 / 2 = 12 square centimetres.
