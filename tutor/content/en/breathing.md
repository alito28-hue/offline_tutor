The respiratory system takes in oxygen, which the body needs, and gets rid of carbon dioxide, a waste product. In humans, the lungs are the main organ for breathing.

Breathing has two movements. Step 1, inhaling: the diaphragm is pulled downward, the lungs expand and air comes in. Step 2, exhaling: air rich in carbon dioxide goes out of the body.

Air goes through the nose and mouth, then the larynx, then down the trachea and into the lungs. It reaches tiny air sacs called alveoli. In the alveoli, oxygen moves into the blood and carbon dioxide moves out of the blood.
