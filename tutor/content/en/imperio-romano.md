The Roman Empire was the stage of Roman civilization after the Republic, when power was concentrated in the emperor.

At its peak it went from the Atlantic Ocean in the west to the Caspian and Red Seas in the east, and from the Sahara Desert in the south to the Rhine and Danube rivers in the north.

Its culture had a strong influence on the language, religion, architecture, literature and laws of the lands it ruled.
