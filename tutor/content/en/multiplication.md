Multiplication finds the product of two numbers. For example, three times five is the total of five threes added together, or of three fives. It is written 3 × 5 = 15.

You can picture multiplication as tiles in a rectangle: one number is the tiles along one side and the other number is the tiles along the other side. A rectangle of 3 rows with 5 tiles in each row has 3 × 5 = 15 tiles.

Multiplication is the opposite of division. If 3 × 5 = 15, then 15 / 5 = 3. Multiplication also gives the area of a rectangle: multiply the size of one side by the size of the other side.
