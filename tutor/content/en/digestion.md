The digestive system is the part of the body that digests food. It breaks food down into simple chemicals that can be absorbed into the blood. It also gets rid of waste.

Food travels through the digestive system in order. Step 1: food enters at the mouth and goes down the oesophagus. Step 2: in the stomach, gastric acid is added, and a layer of mucus protects the stomach from the acid. Step 3: food moves through the small intestine and then the large intestine, and waste leaves the body.

Digestion also needs digestive enzymes, which break down food into substances the body can absorb. From the blood, nutrients go to the liver, which adjusts the mix to what the body needs. The food then feeds the body and helps it repair itself.
