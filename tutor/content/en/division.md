Division is the opposite of multiplication. For example, 6 / 3 means 6 divided by 3, and the answer is 2, because 2 × 3 = 6.

The numbers in a division have names. The number being divided is the dividend, the number you divide by is the divisor and the answer is the quotient. In 6 / 3 = 2, the dividend is 6, the divisor is 3 and the quotient is 2.

In division, any amount left over is called the remainder. For example, 14 / 4 gives a quotient of 3 with a remainder of 2. To check it, step 1: multiply 3 × 4 = 12. Step 2: add the remainder, 12 + 2 = 14. Division by zero is undefined, so you cannot divide by zero.
