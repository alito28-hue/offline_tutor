Mount Everest is the highest mountain on Earth, at 8,848.86 meters above sea level.

It is in Asia, in the Himalayas, and it marks the border between China and Nepal.

It has several names: in Tibet it is called Chomolungma, which means "mother of the universe", and in Nepal, Sagarmatha, "the forehead of the sky".
