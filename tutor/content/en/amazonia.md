The Amazon is a region of South America with the tropical forest of the Amazon River basin. It is the largest tropical forest in the world.

It covers about 7 million square kilometers, shared among nine territories. Brazil and Peru have the largest part.

The others are Colombia, Bolivia, Venezuela, Ecuador, Guyana, Suriname and French Guiana.
