Alexander the Great was king of Macedon, an ancient Greek kingdom. He was born in 356 BC and died in Babylon in 323 BC.

He succeeded his father, Philip II, in 336 BC, at the age of twenty.

By age thirty he had formed one of the largest empires of antiquity, from the Ionian Sea to northwest India.
