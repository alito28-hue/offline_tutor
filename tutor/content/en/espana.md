Spain is a country in southwestern Europe, with territories in northern Africa. It is one of the 27 states of the European Union. Its capital and most populated city is Madrid.

Its government is a parliamentary monarchy. It is organized into 17 autonomous communities and 2 autonomous cities.

It covers most of the Iberian Peninsula. It also includes the Balearic Islands, in the Mediterranean Sea, and the Canary Islands, in the Atlantic Ocean.
