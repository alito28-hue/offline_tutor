Mexico is a country in the south of North America. Its capital and most populated city is Mexico City.

It is a federal republic made up of 32 entities: 31 states and the capital.

It borders the United States to the north and Guatemala and Belize to the south. It has coasts on the Pacific Ocean, the Gulf of Mexico and the Caribbean Sea.
