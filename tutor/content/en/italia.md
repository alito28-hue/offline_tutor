Italy is a country in southern Europe and one of the 27 members of the European Union. Its capital and most populated city is Rome.

It lies in the center of the Mediterranean Sea. It covers the Italian Peninsula, the Po Valley and the islands of Sicily and Sardinia, plus about 800 smaller islands.

It is a parliamentary republic made up of 20 regions.
