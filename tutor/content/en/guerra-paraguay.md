The War of the Triple Alliance, or Paraguayan War, set Paraguay against an alliance of Brazil, Uruguay and Argentina, between 1864 and 1870.

Historians debate its causes. Some stress the interests of the British Empire, and others the policies of Francisco Solano Lopez, the president of Paraguay.

Paraguay lost a large part of its population, especially adult men.
