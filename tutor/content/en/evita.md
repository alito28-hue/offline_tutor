Eva Perón, known as Evita, was born in Los Toldos in 1919 and died in Buenos Aires in 1952. She was an actress and politician, and the wife of President Perón.

She worked for the rights of workers, the elderly, children and women.

People in Argentina still have very different opinions about her.
