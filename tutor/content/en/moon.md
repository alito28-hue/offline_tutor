The Moon is the only natural satellite of Earth. It is about one-fourth of the width of Earth. It is a rocky and dusty place with no atmosphere, so there is no air resistance and a feather falls as fast as a hammer.

The phases of the Moon happen because the Moon is round and half of it is lit by the Sun. As the Moon goes around the Earth, we see different amounts of the lit half, so the Moon seems to change shape. The full cycle of phases takes about a month.

The gravity on the Moon is one-sixth of the gravity of Earth, so an object is one-sixth as heavy on the Moon as on Earth.
