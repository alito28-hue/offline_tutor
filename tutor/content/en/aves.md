Birds are vertebrate animals with a body covered in feathers, wings in place of front legs and a beak without teeth.

They lay eggs and keep them warm until the young hatch. There are more than 11,000 living species.

Their size goes from 6.4 centimeters, in the bee hummingbird, to 2.74 meters, in the ostrich.
