Mesopotamia is a historical region of the Near East, between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers.

It roughly matches the non-desert areas of present-day Iraq and part of Syria.

In ancient times it was divided into Assyria in the north and Babylonia in the south. Cities such as Ur and Nippur were there.
