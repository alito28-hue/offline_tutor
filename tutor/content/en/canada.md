Canada is a country in the north of North America. It stretches from the Atlantic Ocean in the east to the Pacific Ocean in the west.

Its capital is Ottawa, and its most populated city is Toronto, followed by Montreal and Vancouver.

It is a federal parliamentary monarchy with 10 provinces and 3 territories, and it has about 40 million people.
