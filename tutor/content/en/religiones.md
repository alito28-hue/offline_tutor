A religion is a system of beliefs, practices, texts and sacred places that connects people with the spiritual or the divine.

Many religions have their own forms of organization and their own scriptures. There is no single definition accepted by everyone.

Some of those with the most followers are Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism and Judaism.
