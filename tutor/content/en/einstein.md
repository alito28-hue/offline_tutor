Albert Einstein was born in Ulm, Germany, in 1879 and died in Princeton, United States, in 1955. He was a physicist.

He is best known for developing the theory of relativity. He is considered the most important scientist of the 20th century.

In 1905, while working in a patent office in Bern, he published his theory of special relativity.
