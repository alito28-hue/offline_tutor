The Guarani are a group of peoples of South America. They live in part of Paraguay, in northeastern Argentina, in southern and southwestern Brazil and in southeastern Bolivia.

In Argentina they live in provinces such as Corrientes, Misiones and Formosa.

In Corrientes, Guarani has been an alternative official language since 2004.
