The Malvinas Islands, called the Falkland Islands in English, are an archipelago of South America in the Argentine Sea, about 500 kilometers from the coast.

Argentina claims sovereignty over them, as set out in its Constitution; the United Kingdom has occupied them since 1833.

They had no Indigenous population: their first visitors were Europeans, and several countries tried to settle there.
