The Olympic Games are the largest international sports event. Athletes from more than two hundred countries take part.

There are two kinds: the Summer and the Winter Games, held two years apart. Each kind takes place every four years.

They were inspired by the games of ancient Greece, in the city of Olympia. The modern version came about through the efforts of the Frenchman Pierre de Coubertin.
