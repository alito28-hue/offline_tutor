The Convention on the Rights of the Child is an international treaty of the United Nations, signed on November 20, 1989.

It says that children have the same rights as adults and also need special protection because they are still growing.

It brings together their civil, political, social, economic and cultural rights in one text. The states that join it promise to follow it.
