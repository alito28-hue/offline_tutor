A rainbow is an arc of colors that appears in the sky when sunlight passes through drops of water.

As it passes through the drops, the light bends and separates into its colors. This bending is called refraction.

According to Isaac Newton, it has seven colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet. Red is on the outside and violet on the inside.
