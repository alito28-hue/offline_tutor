An adjective is a word that goes with a noun and says what it is like. For example, in "big book", big is the adjective.

It can say something you notice with your senses, like green or big, or something you think, like difficult.

When you add an adjective, you are no longer talking about just anything. "A green book" is not any book: it is one that is green.
