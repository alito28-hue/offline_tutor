South Africa is a country in southern Africa, with coasts on the Atlantic and Indian oceans.

Its capital has a special status, because it is made up of three cities: Pretoria, seat of the executive power; Bloemfontein, of the judicial power; and Cape Town, of the legislative power.

The most populated city is Johannesburg. The country is organized into nine provinces.
