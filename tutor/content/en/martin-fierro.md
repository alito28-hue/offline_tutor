The Gaucho Martín Fierro is a narrative poem in verse, written by the Argentine José Hernández in 1872. It is an example of the gaucho genre.

Martín Fierro is a hard-working gaucho. Social injustice turns him into an outlaw gaucho.

It has a sequel, The Return of Martín Fierro, from 1879. Today the two parts are known together as Martín Fierro.
