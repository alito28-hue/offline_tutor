The water cycle is the path water follows on Earth. Step one is evaporation: the Sun heats water and it turns into a gas called water vapour.

Step two of the water cycle is condensation: when water vapour gets cold it becomes tiny liquid droplets, and groups of droplets make clouds. The droplets are so light that rising air keeps them up.

Step three of the water cycle is precipitation: droplets join together and become heavier, until rising air cannot hold them, so water falls as rain, snow, sleet or hail. Then the water collects in lakes, oceans or under the ground, and it evaporates again.
