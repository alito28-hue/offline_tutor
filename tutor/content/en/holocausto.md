The Holocaust, or Shoah, was the genocide that Nazi Germany committed against the Jews of Europe and other groups during the Second World War.

It grew out of a racist idea that the Nazis put into practice in the lands they occupied.

About six million Jews died, victims of a systematic plan, along with Roma people, people with disabilities and other groups.
