A multiple of a number is the result of multiplying that number by another whole number.

To find the multiples of a number, multiply it by 1, by 2, by 3 and so on.

Example: 5, 10, 15 and 20 are multiples of 5, because 5 × 1 = 5, 5 × 2 = 10, 5 × 3 = 15 and 5 × 4 = 20.
