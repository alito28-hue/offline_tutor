A number is a divisor of another if the remainder is zero when you divide.

Example: 3 is a divisor of 6, because 6 ÷ 3 = 2 with nothing left over. But 4 is not a divisor of 6, because there is something left over.

Every natural number can be divided by 1 and by itself.
