The Solar System is a group of space objects held together by gravity, with the Sun in the center. The Sun is a huge ball of hot glowing gas that gives off light and heat. Everything else moves around the Sun.

The Solar System has eight main planets, including Earth, and many moons that orbit them. It also has dwarf planets like Pluto, asteroids and comets.
