Argentina's national parks protect the nature and the cultural heritage of the country.

The first was Nahuel Huapi. It was created in 1922 under another name, Southern National Park, and took its current name in 1934.

In 1934, Iguazu National Park and the National Parks Administration, which looks after them, were also created.
