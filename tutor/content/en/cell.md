The cell is the basic structure of living things. All living things are made up of cells, from the organs in the human body to the parts of plants. All cells are made by the division of cells that already exist.

The cell membrane separates the inside of the cell from the outside. Inside some cells there are separate parts called organelles, which do different jobs. For example, the nucleus is where the DNA is, and mitochondria convert chemical energy.

There are two types of cell. Prokaryotes, such as bacteria, are simple cells with no nucleus. Eukaryotes are more complex cells with a nucleus. There are around 37 trillion cells in the human body.
