Leonardo da Vinci was a genius of the Italian Renaissance. He was born in Vinci in 1452 and died in Amboise, France, in 1519.

He was a painter, anatomist, architect, botanist, writer, sculptor, philosopher, engineer, inventor, musician and poet.

He studied with the painter Andrea del Verrocchio. He worked in Milan, Rome, Bologna and Venice, and he spent his last years in France, invited by King Francis I.
