The May Revolution was a series of events in Buenos Aires, then the capital of the Viceroyalty of the Rio de la Plata, which depended on the King of Spain.

They took place during May Week, from May 18 to May 25, 1810. On May 25 the First Junta, a new government, was sworn in.

It began the path toward the Argentine state, but it was not a declaration of independence: the Junta still governed in the name of King Ferdinand VII.
