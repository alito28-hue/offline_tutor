A volcano is a mountain that has lava, which is hot liquid rock, coming out from a magma chamber under the ground. Most volcanoes have a crater at the top. Volcanoes are formed by the movement of tectonic plates.

A volcano erupts in order. Step 1: magma collects under the ground and pressure builds up. Step 2: when there is enough pressure, the volcano erupts. Step 3: lava, steam, gases, ash and broken rock come out, usually at the top and sometimes at the side.

Volcanoes can also form away from plate boundaries, at hotspots such as Hawaii. Volcanoes are found on other planets too, such as Olympus Mons on Mars. Scientists who study volcanoes are called volcanologists.
