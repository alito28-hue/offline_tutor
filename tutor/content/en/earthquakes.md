An earthquake is when the tectonic plates of the Earth shake and move its surface. Strong earthquakes damage buildings. People who study earthquakes are called seismologists.

Earthquakes are caused by disturbances in the Earth. Step 1: tectonic plates move slowly and get stuck, so tension builds up. Step 2: the plates suddenly break free. Step 3: waves of energy travel through the Earth. The first point of the break is the hypocenter or focus, and the point on the ground above it is the epicenter.

A seismometer detects the shaking of an earthquake. The strength of an earthquake can be measured with the Richter scale. The largest earthquake ever measured had a magnitude of 9.5.
