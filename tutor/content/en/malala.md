Malala Yousafzai was born in Mingora, Pakistan, in 1997. She is an activist for the education of women and girls.

She is the youngest person ever to receive the Nobel Peace Prize. She won it in 2014, at age 17.

She defends the right to education in her home district of Swat, where girls were at times banned from going to school.
