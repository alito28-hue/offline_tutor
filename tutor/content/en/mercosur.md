Mercosur, the Southern Common Market, is an economic bloc of South American countries.

It was created in 1991 by the Treaty of Asuncion, signed by Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay and Uruguay.

Today it also includes Bolivia and Venezuela, although Venezuela has been suspended since 2016. Other countries, such as Chile and Colombia, are associated states.
