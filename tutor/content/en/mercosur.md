Mercosur, the Southern Common Market, is an economic bloc of countries in South America.

It was created in 1991 by the Treaty of Asuncion, signed by Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay and Uruguay. Bolivia has been a full member since November 2023.

Venezuela joined in 2012 and has been suspended since 2016. Other countries, such as Chile and Colombia, are associated states. The list of members can change over time.
