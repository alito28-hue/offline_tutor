Japan is a country in East Asia, in the northwest Pacific Ocean. It is an archipelago of 14,125 islands.

Its five main islands are Hokkaido, Honshu, Shikoku, Kyushu and Okinawa. The capital and largest city is Tokyo.

About three quarters of the country is mountains. That is why its 126 million people live mostly on narrow plains near the coast.
