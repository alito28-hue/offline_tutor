Kenya is a country in East Africa, with a coast on the Indian Ocean. Its capital is Nairobi.

It borders Ethiopia, Somalia, Tanzania, Uganda and South Sudan. It had 47.6 million people in 2019 and officially recognizes 44 ethnic groups.

Its name comes from Mount Kenya, the second highest mountain in Africa. The country is also known for its biodiversity.
