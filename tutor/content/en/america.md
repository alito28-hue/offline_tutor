The Americas form the second largest continent on Earth, after Asia. They cover much of the Western Hemisphere.

They go from the Arctic Ocean in the north to the Diego Ramirez Islands in the south. The Atlantic and Pacific oceans border them to the east and west.

They have more than 43 million square kilometers and about 12.5% of the human population.
