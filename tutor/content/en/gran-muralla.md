The Great Wall is an ancient Chinese fortification. It was built and rebuilt between the 5th century BC and the 16th century to protect the northern border of the Chinese Empire.

With all its branches it measures about 21,200 kilometers, although only 30% survives today.

On average it is 6 to 7 meters high and 4 to 5 meters wide. It has been a World Heritage Site since 1987.
