Abolitionism argues for ending the laws and customs of slavery.

Túpac Amaru II was the first to decree its abolition, in 1780, but it was not carried out because his rebellion was defeated. Haiti was the first country to abolish it completely.

In Argentina, the Assembly of the Year XIII declared the freedom of children born to enslaved mothers, and the Constitution of 1853 abolished slavery.
