Apartheid was a system of racial segregation in South Africa and Namibia, between 1948 and 1991.

It separated people by their color in housing, schools and places of leisure, and only white people had the power to vote.

It aimed to keep power in the hands of the white minority, 21% of the population. Nelson Mandela was one of the leaders of the struggle against apartheid.
