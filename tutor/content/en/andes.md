The Andes mountain range runs along the west of South America, by the coast of the Pacific Ocean.

It crosses Argentina, Chile, Bolivia, Peru, Ecuador, Colombia and Venezuela. It is more than 8,500 kilometers long: the longest continental mountain range on Earth.

Its highest point is Aconcagua, at 6,962 meters. It is the highest mountain outside Asia. The Andes also have the highest volcanoes on the planet.
